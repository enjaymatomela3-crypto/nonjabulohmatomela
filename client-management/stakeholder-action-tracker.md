# Stakeholder Action Tracker

| Action | Owner | Date Requested | Due Date | Status | Next Follow-Up |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Example action | Stakeholder | 01/09/2026 | 04/09/2026 | Awaiting response | 03/09/2026 |

## Status options

- Not started
- In progress
- Awaiting customer
- Awaiting internal response
- Blocked
- Complete

## Principle

An outstanding task should have an owner, a status and a next action. This prevents requests from simply sitting unanswered.

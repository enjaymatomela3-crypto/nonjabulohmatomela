These activities translate directly into client success and onboarding environments.

## Case Study: Stakeholder Coordination

### Situation

A deliverable required contributions from several people with different responsibilities.

### Action

I coordinated requests and followed up on outstanding information while keeping track of what had been received and what was still required.

### Result

The process remained organised, responsibilities were clearer, and outstanding actions were less likely to be overlooked.

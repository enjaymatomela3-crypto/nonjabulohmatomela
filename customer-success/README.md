# 🤝 Customer Success

## Overview

Customer success is about helping customers achieve the outcome they expected when they chose a service or product.

My transferable experience centres on communication, follow-up, problem solving, stakeholder coordination and making complex information easier to understand.

---

## My Customer Success Approach

### 1. Understand
Listen carefully to the customer's request and identify the actual outcome they need.

### 2. Clarify
Confirm requirements and remove ambiguity before work progresses.

### 3. Act
Complete the task or coordinate the person who can resolve it.

### 4. Follow Up
Do not allow an unanswered question or outstanding action to disappear.

### 5. Close the Loop
Confirm that the customer has what they need and understands the next step.

---

## Case Study: Proactive Follow-Up

### Situation

Projects can stall when information, decisions or responses are outstanding from another person.

### My approach

Rather than allowing an outstanding request to sit passively, I use a simple follow-up process:

- Record what is outstanding
- Identify the responsible person
- Set an appropriate follow-up point
- Send a concise reminder
- Clarify exactly what is still needed
- Escalate if the delay affects a deadline
- Update the relevant stakeholders

### Customer Success relevance

This demonstrates:
- Ownership
- Proactive communication
- Organisation
- Expectation management
- Issue prevention
- Customer-focused follow-through

---

## Communication Style

My written communication aims to be:

**Clear** — the recipient should understand the request.

**Concise** — unnecessary information is removed.

**Action-oriented** — the recipient knows what needs to happen next.

**Professional** — communication remains respectful even when following up on delays.

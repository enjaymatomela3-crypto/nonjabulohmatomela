# Customer Follow-Up Framework

## Purpose

A follow-up should make it easy for the recipient to understand:

1. What is outstanding
2. Why it is needed
3. What action is required
4. When it is needed
5. What happens next

## Example structure

**Subject:** Follow-up: [Action / Requirement]

Hi [Name],

I’m following up regarding [specific item].

To keep the process moving, could you please provide/confirm [specific action]?

If there is anything preventing this from being completed, please let me know and I can help clarify the requirement.

Once this is received, the next step will be [next step].

Thank you,
Nonjabulo

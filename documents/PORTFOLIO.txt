7. CLIENT COMMUNICATION SAMPLES
The following are illustrative samples written to demonstrate my communication style and approach — not excerpts from an actual client account.
Sample 1 — Missing Information Follow-Up (Email)
Dear [Client Name],
Thank you for the information provided so far.
To progress to the next stage, we still require the following:
—	[Required item 1]
—	[Required item 2]
—	[Required item 3]
Once these items have been received, we can proceed with the next stage of the process.
If you are unsure about any of the requested information, please let me know and I would be happy to clarify what is required.
Kind regards,
Nonjabulo Matomela

Sample 2 — Explaining a Process (Client Guide)
1	Initial Information
We collect the information required to understand your needs and set up your account or service.
2	Review
We review the information provided and identify anything that may need clarification.
3	Outstanding Items
If additional information is required, we let you know exactly what is needed and why.
4	Setup
Once the required information has been received, we proceed with the relevant setup process.
5	Orientation
We explain the next steps and make sure you know where to find information and support.
6	Handover
Once onboarding is complete, you move into the normal service/support process.

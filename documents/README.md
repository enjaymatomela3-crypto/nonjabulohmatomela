# 📄 Professional Documents

Place your final, recruiter-ready documents in this folder.

Recommended files:

- `CV.pdf`
- `Portfolio.pdf`

## Important

Only upload documents that you are permitted to share publicly.

Remove:
- Client names where confidentiality applies
- Personal information that is not needed
- Signatures
- Phone numbers belonging to other people
- Confidential project information
- Proprietary company material
- Unpublished or restricted data

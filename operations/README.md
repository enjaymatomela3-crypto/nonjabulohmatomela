# ⚙️ Operations & Process Coordination

## Overview

Strong customer operations depend on reliable processes behind the customer experience.

My experience has developed skills in organising information, tracking tasks, coordinating deadlines, maintaining documentation and identifying what needs attention.

---

## Process I Use for Outstanding Tasks

### Capture
Record the task, request or missing information.

### Assign
Identify who is responsible for the next action.

### Prioritise
Determine whether the task affects a deadline, customer or dependent activity.

### Follow Up
Contact the responsible person at an appropriate time.

### Escalate
If a delay creates a material problem, raise it through the appropriate channel.

### Close
Confirm completion and update the relevant record.

---

## Quality Checklist

Before considering a task complete, I check:

- Has the customer's requirement been addressed?
- Is the information complete?
- Has the correct person been informed?
- Are there outstanding actions?
- Is the next step clear?
- Has the relevant documentation been updated?

# Customer Onboarding Checklist

A simple example of a structured onboarding workflow.

## Before onboarding
- [ ] Customer details confirmed
- [ ] Primary contact confirmed
- [ ] Requirements understood
- [ ] Required information identified
- [ ] Timeline confirmed

## During onboarding
- [ ] Welcome / introduction sent
- [ ] Required information requested
- [ ] Customer questions addressed
- [ ] Missing items tracked
- [ ] Internal actions assigned
- [ ] Blockers identified
- [ ] Customer updated

## Completion
- [ ] All required information received
- [ ] Outstanding issues resolved
- [ ] Customer informed of completion
- [ ] Handover completed
- [ ] Next steps communicated

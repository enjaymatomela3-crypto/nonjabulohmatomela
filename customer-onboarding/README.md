# 🚀 Customer Onboarding

## Overview

This section demonstrates how my existing experience can transfer to customer onboarding work.

Customer onboarding requires a structured process: understand the customer's needs, collect required information, communicate next steps, monitor progress, identify blockers and ensure a smooth handover or completion.

---

## Example Onboarding Workflow

**1. Welcome**
- Confirm the customer's requirements
- Introduce the process
- Set expectations

**2. Information Collection**
- Identify required information
- Request missing documents or details
- Check information for completeness

**3. Setup / Coordination**
- Coordinate internal or external stakeholders
- Track outstanding actions
- Keep the customer informed

**4. Blocker Management**
- Identify delays or missing information
- Follow up proactively
- Escalate where necessary

**5. Completion**
- Confirm requirements have been met
- Communicate completion
- Provide next steps or handover information

---

## Case Study: Coordinating a Multi-Stakeholder Deliverable

### Situation

A professional project required information and inputs from multiple parties before a final deliverable could be completed.

### My approach

I:
- Identified what information was outstanding
- Communicated requests clearly
- Followed up with the relevant people
- Tracked progress toward the required deliverable
- Clarified requirements where necessary
- Kept the process moving rather than allowing unanswered requests to sit indefinitely

### Customer Success relevance

This demonstrates:
- Proactive follow-up
- Stakeholder coordination
- Requirement gathering
- Deadline awareness
- Clear written communication
- Blocker identification
- Ownership of next steps

---

## Onboarding Principles

I approach onboarding around four questions:

1. **What does the customer need?**
2. **What information or action is required from them?**
3. **What could prevent them from progressing?**
4. **What is the next action, who owns it, and when is it due?**
